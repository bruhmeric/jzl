Why JAZZEL

Built to your sizes, not to a catalogue

Every piece from the Matale workshop is made to order — the material, the finish and the fit are yours to choose.

Premium materials

Kiln-dried teak, melamine and MDF boards, steel frames and upholstery chosen to last in island weather.

Customizable designs

Any piece is sized to your room — change the width, finish, fabric or configuration before it is built.

Ergonomic comfort

Seat heights, depths and back angles chosen for long evenings, not just for the photograph.

Space-saving designs

Sliding wardrobes, wall-hung units and beds with storage for Colombo apartments and village homes alike.

Easy assembly

Flat-packed with clear instructions and the tools included — or our islandwide team assembles it for you.

Sustainable practices

Plantation-grown timber and board offcuts recycled through the workshop, nothing wasted to landfill.

The workshop

Made in Matale, finished by hand

Boards are cut, sanded, primed and lacquered in our own workshop — then checked piece by piece before the van leaves.

Melamine Board

Scratch-resistant, wipe-clean surfaces for wardrobes and kids' rooms.

MDF Board

Painted and profiled doors, drawer fronts and headboards.

Steel Frame

Powder-coated frames for office desks, beds and iron cabinets.
